## 2. Categories of Data Processed, Purposes and Legal Bases

VinoInvest processes only the categories of data necessary to provide the service. The details for each category are set out below.

### 2.1 Registration and account data
- **Data processed:** email address and password. Authentication is handled through the provider Supabase; the password is stored in encrypted (hashed) form and is never accessible in plain text to the Controller.
- **Purpose:** creation and management of the account, authentication, provision of the subscription service.
- **Legal basis:** performance of a contract to which the data subject is a party (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### 2.2 Usage data
- **Data processed:** wines viewed, searches performed, interface preferences (theme, currency, language), progress in Academy content, user-interface state.
- **Purpose:** operation and personalisation of the service, improvement of the user experience.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and the Controller's legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the Platform (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### 2.3 Payment data
- **Data processed:** payment-card and transaction data are processed **exclusively** by the payment providers Stripe and PayPal. **VinoInvest does not collect, display or store full payment-card data.** The Controller only receives information on the outcome of the transaction and on the status of the subscription.
- **Purpose:** management of payments and subscriptions, accounting and tax obligations.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and compliance with legal obligations regarding tax and accounting (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

### 2.4 Transactional email
- **Data processed:** email address and content of service communications (confirmations, price alerts, password reset, subscription-related communications). Delivery is handled through the provider Resend.
- **Purpose:** sending of service communications necessary for the use of the Platform (not marketing).
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### 2.5 Technical logs
- **Data processed:** IP address, browser and device type, access timestamps, server diagnostic data.
- **Purpose:** security of the Platform, prevention of abuse and fraud, technical diagnostics.
- **Legal basis:** the Controller's legitimate interest in the security of its systems (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 6. Data Subject Rights (Articles 15-22 GDPR)

Within the limits and under the conditions provided by the GDPR, the data subject has the right to exercise the following rights:

- **Access (Art. 15):** obtain confirmation of processing and a copy of the data concerning them;
- **Rectification (Art. 16):** obtain correction of inaccurate data or completion of incomplete data;
- **Erasure / right to be forgotten (Art. 17):** obtain the erasure of the data;
- **Restriction (Art. 18):** obtain restriction of processing;
- **Portability (Art. 20):** receive the data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format;
- **Objection (Art. 21):** object to processing based on legitimate interest;
- **Right not to be subject to automated decision-making (Art. 22):** VinoInvest does not make decisions producing legal effects based solely on automated processing.

To exercise these rights, please write to [EMAIL CONTATTO]. The Controller responds without undue delay and in any case within 30 days of the request.

## 7. Right to Lodge a Complaint

Without prejudice to any other administrative or judicial remedy, a data subject who considers that their rights have been infringed has the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. Data subjects resident in other EU Member States may lodge a complaint with their local supervisory authority.
