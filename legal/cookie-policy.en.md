## 2. What cookies and similar technologies are

**Cookies** are small text files that websites store on the user's device to keep information, for example to maintain an active authentication session.

Technologies similar to cookies, such as the browser's **localStorage**, allow information to be stored on the user's device in a comparable way. VinoInvest mainly uses localStorage to store user preferences and consent.

These technologies may be:
- **necessary/technical**, indispensable for the operation of the service;
- **non-necessary** (e.g. profiling, marketing, third-party analytics), which require the user's consent.

VinoInvest uses **only** necessary technical tools. The complete list is set out below.

| Name / Type | Purpose | Provider | Duration |

## 5. Legal basis

The technical/necessary cookies and technologies listed above are **exempt from the consent requirement**, as they are strictly necessary to provide the service explicitly requested by the user, as provided by Article 122 of Legislative Decree 196/2003 and by the Garante's Guidelines of 10 June 2021. For them, the legal basis of any related data processing is the Controller's legitimate interest / the performance of the contract (Art. 6 GDPR).

Since VinoInvest does not use non-necessary cookies, no prior consent is required for profiling, marketing or analytics purposes.

## 6. Managing and withdrawing preferences

Users can manage their choices at any time:

- **Cookie banner:** on the first visit an information banner is shown with the option to accept or continue with essential cookies only. The choice is stored in the `vino_cookie_consent_v1` localStorage entry.
- **Browser settings:** users can block or delete cookies and site data through their browser settings. Disabling technical cookies/tokens may impair the correct operation of the service (for example account login).
- **Clearing localStorage:** users can clear the data stored in localStorage through their browser functions; this will cause the banner to be shown again and default preferences to be restored.
